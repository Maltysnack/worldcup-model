How it works

Ratings. Elo over every international since 1990. Everyone starts at 1500; higher is stronger.

Fixtures. A goals model turns the rating gap into win, draw and loss chances. Home sides get +25 at non-neutral venues.

Tournaments. Current Elo plus a small overlay (past honours, host, altitude), then thousands of simulated tournaments. The overlay is unproven: on 2026 its effects were within noise.

Bot. A paper-money test of plain Elo against Sportsbet prices. The rules and every bet are under Rules and ledger, at the bottom of the page.
